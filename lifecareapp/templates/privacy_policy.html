Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy outlines how Lifecare Connect handles your data in compliance with the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya).

1. Introduction

Lifecare Connect ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our platform.

2. Data We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide to us when you register on our platform, create a profile, or book services. This includes:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, phone number, physical address.
  • Demographic Information: Date of birth, gender, county.
  • Sensitive Personal Data (Health Data): Medical conditions, allergies, preferred care type, and medical history documents.
  • Professional Information (for Doctors): License number, qualifications, expertise, and charge rates.

3. Purpose of Processing

We process your data for the following purposes:

  • To facilitate healthcare service delivery and homecare connections.
  • To verify the credentials of healthcare providers.
  • To process payments via M-Pesa.
  • To communicate with you regarding appointments and updates.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations under Kenyan law.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your data based on your explicit consent. By checking the consent box during registration, you agree to the collection and processing of your personal and health data for the purposes stated above.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to secure your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. This includes encryption of sensitive documents and restricted access to health records.

6. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not sell your data. We may share your information with:

  • Healthcare Providers/Patients: To facilitate the services you have requested.
  • Payment Processors: Such as Safaricom (M-Pesa) to process transactions.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law to comply with legal processes.

7. Cross-Border Transfers

Your data is primarily stored and processed within Kenya. If any data is transferred outside the country, we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place as required by Section 50 of the Data Protection Act.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

9. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request the deletion of your personal data (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your data for specific purposes.
  • Data Portability: Request the transfer of your data to another service provider.
